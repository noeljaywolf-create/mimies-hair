Mimies Hair,
Built To Book.

A premium salon website that looks expensive, loads fast on mobile data, and turns visitors into WhatsApp bookings.

The Brief

A Salon That
Felt Premium.

Mimies Hair is a beauty salon in Tembisa, Johannesburg offering hair styling, braids, nails and makeup. The challenge: build a website that matched the red-carpet quality of the work - without scaring away local clients on expensive data plans or slow connections.

The result is a five-page experience in a cinematic black and gold palette, built from scratch with vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript - no heavy page builders, no bloated frameworks, no unnecessary plugins.

The Design

Black & Gold,
Everywhere That Counts.

Gold #CA8A04 Ink #0C0A09 Champagne #E9C15B Muted #7C6F5E

Playfair Display for editorial headlines, Inter for crisp body text, and a restrained gold accent used sparingly - luxury through restraint. Every section earns its place: a 3D hero, a signature reel, service carousels, a price book and a booking-first contact page.

The Build

Fast, Light,
Findable.

Hand-coded front-end with GSAP scroll animations, shipped free on GitHub Pages. Performance was treated as a feature: every image re-encoded, background videos swapped for a 0.3 MB loop, fonts preconnected and images lazy-loaded with aspect-ratio reserved to prevent layout shift.

SEO & Conversion

Found On Google,
Booked On WhatsApp.

Local search is wired in with HairSalon structured data, a sitemap and location-focused copy so the salon shows up for "hair salon in Tembisa". Every booking path - the form, the price cards, the floating button - funnels straight into WhatsApp, where the salon already talks to clients.

HairSalon Schema Name, hours, geo and prices for rich Google results.
WhatsApp-First The booking form opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message.
